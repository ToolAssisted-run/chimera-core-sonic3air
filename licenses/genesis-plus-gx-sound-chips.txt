The Mega Drive sound chips in the Oxygen engine (Sonic 3 A.I.R.)
==============================================================

Two files of Sonic 3 A.I.R.'s Oxygen engine emulate the Mega Drive's sound
chips, and both are, in upstream's own words, "a derivative work ... based on
the Genesis Plus GX source code":

  Oxygen/oxygenengine/source/oxygen/simulation/sound/sn76489.cpp (and .h)
      the PSG (SN76489). Its header carries the Genesis Plus GX licence below,
      by Eke-Eke: free to use and redistribute, but never sold or used in a
      commercial product or activity, with the complete source of a modified
      copy, and with this notice.

  Oxygen/oxygenengine/source/oxygen/simulation/sound/ym2612.cpp (and .h)
      the FM chip (YM2612), MAME's fm.c by Jarek Burczynski and Tatsuyuki
      Satoh with Eke-Eke's changes for Genesis Plus GX. Its header names its
      authors and states no licence of its own; Genesis Plus GX's changes are
      under Genesis Plus GX's terms.

Upstream marks both files as published under the GNU GPLv3 as well. The rest
of Sonic 3 A.I.R. is under the GNU GPLv3 alone (LICENSE). See this
repository's LICENSE for what that means for a package built from here.
